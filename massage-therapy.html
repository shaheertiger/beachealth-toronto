How Massage Therapy Works

Your therapist starts with a short assessment of your symptoms and goals, then adjusts techniques and pressure to suit your body and your comfort.

Treatment may include:

  • Deep tissue therapy: Slow, focused pressure to reach deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue.
  • Myofascial release & trigger point therapy: Releasing tight bands and knots that restrict movement or refer pain.
  • Sports massage: Supporting training, recovery and return to sport after injury.
  • Prenatal massage: Comfortable, safely positioned treatment for pregnancy-related aches and tension.
  • TMJ & jaw massage: Focused work for jaw clenching, jaw pain and related headaches.

Conditions We Commonly Help With

  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Low back pain
  • Tension headaches
  • Postural strain from desk work
  • Sports injuries and training recovery
  • Stress and muscle tension
  • Pregnancy-related discomfort
  • Jaw tension and TMJ symptoms

You do not need an injury to benefit — many clients book regular massage to manage stress and stay ahead of tension.

Your Comfort Comes First

You are always in control of your treatment. Your therapist will check in on pressure, and you can ask for more or less at any time. You will be draped with sheets throughout, with only the area being treated uncovered.

Our massage therapists work alongside Beachealth’s osteopaths and chiropractor, so soft-tissue treatment can complement your other care when that helps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I receive an RMT receipt for insurance?

Yes. Our therapists are Registered Massage Therapists, so you receive an official receipt you can submit to your extended health plan. Coverage amounts vary by plan.

What should I wear?

You can undress to your comfort level. You will be covered with sheets and only the area being worked on is uncovered. If you prefer to stay clothed, wear loose, comfortable clothing.

Will deep tissue massage hurt?

Deep work can feel intense in tight areas, but it should never be more than you are comfortable with. Tell your therapist at any point and they will adjust the pressure.

How long are appointments?

Several appointment lengths are available — you can see the options and choose one when you book online.

Can I book massage during pregnancy?

Yes. Prenatal massage uses supportive positioning for comfort and safety. Let us know you are pregnant when you book.
