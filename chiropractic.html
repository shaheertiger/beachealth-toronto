Chiropractic Care

Beachealth Services > Chiropractic Care

Chiropractic care at Beachealth is designed to help you reduce pain, move more freely, and feel more confident in your body.

Dr. Adam Bletsoe combines hands-on chiropractic care with movement, contemporary pain science, and nervous-system-informed strategies. The approach can be simple and hands-on when that is what you need — or go deeper when pain has become persistent, recurring, or difficult to understand.

Whether you are dealing with a new problem, an old injury, recurring stiffness, or pain that flares with stress, the goal is the same: help you feel and function better, with a clear path forward.

To book an appointment you can call us at (416) 546-4887 or book online.

How Chiropractic Care at Beachealth Works

Bodies can become stiff, guarded, or painful for many reasons — injury, physical strain, repetitive postures, or a protective nervous system. Care is adapted to your body and your goals.

Treatment may include:

  • Chiropractic adjustments & gentle joint mobilization: Restoring smooth motion to restricted spinal and extremity joints.
  • Instrument-assisted adjusting & soft-tissue therapy: Relieving muscle guarding and myofascial tension without forceful manual twisting.
  • Movement, exercise & rehabilitation: Restoring functional load tolerance, active mobility, and movement confidence.
  • Breathing & nervous-system regulation strategies: Down-regulating protective muscular guarding and chronic autonomic tension.
  • Education & contemporary pain science: Empowering you with a clear, rational understanding of your symptoms and recovery roadmap.

Conditions We Commonly Help With

People come to Dr. Adam for many different reasons, including:

  • Back and neck pain
  • Headaches and migraines
  • Shoulder and joint stiffness
  • Jaw tension and TMJ-related symptoms
  • Sports and exercise-related injuries
  • Recurring injuries & full-body tension
  • Persistent or chronic pain flaring with stress
  • Symptoms not responding to previous treatment

You do not need to know exactly what is causing your pain before coming in — part of your first visit is figuring that out together.

A Different Approach to Persistent or Recurring Pain

Sometimes pain lasts longer than expected. An injury may have healed, imaging may not fully explain the symptoms, treatment may provide only temporary relief, or the pain may move, fluctuate, or become particularly sensitive to stress.

In these situations, Dr. Adam incorporates contemporary pain science, mindfulness, and principles from Pain Reprocessing Therapy (PRT). This does not mean that your pain is imaginary or "all in your head" — pain is 100% real. But sometimes the brain and nervous system learn to stay protective long after protection is necessary.

We help the body move comfortably again while teaching the nervous system that everyday movement and physical sensations are safe. The goal is not simply to treat the painful area repeatedly, but to help you become less protective, more capable, and more confident in your body over time.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first appointment has three coordinated parts:

1 Understand Your Story

We explore your symptoms, medical history, lifestyle factors, and what pain is holding you back from doing. For recurring symptoms, we also look at stress and recovery routines that influence healing.

2 Examine What Is Going On

A comprehensive physical examination to evaluate mobility, nerve dynamics, posture, and joint function, ensuring chiropractic care is safe, appropriate, and beneficial for your condition.

3 Build a Plan Together

We discuss findings, outline collaborative self-care strategies, and begin hands-on treatment when appropriate on day one so you leave feeling informed, supported, and moving easier.

Care That Fits the Person

There isn't one chiropractic treatment that everyone receives. Some people want straightforward hands-on treatment and movement advice. Others prefer gentler adjusting. Athletes may want help restoring movement and getting back to training. And someone who has been dealing with persistent pain for years may benefit from longer appointments that include pain education, movement confidence, and nervous-system work.

The treatment should fit you, rather than forcing you into a predetermined treatment plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does chiropractic care hurt?

Chiropractic care should not hurt. Treatment may occasionally feel briefly sensitive in guarded areas, but most patients experience it as comfortable pressure, stretching, or massage-like relief. Dr. Adam adjusts every technique to your comfort level.

Do I have to be adjusted or "cracked"?

No. Adjustments are always optional. Treatment can also include gentle joint mobilization, instrument-assisted techniques, soft-tissue work, exercises, and pain education. You always have full choice in your care.

What is an adjustment?

An adjustment is a precise, controlled movement applied to a restricted joint to restore natural motion and help your body feel safe moving again. Many people feel an immediate sense of greater mobility or reduced tension afterward.

How long will it take to feel better?

Many patients feel relief within the first few visits. Long-standing conditions may require several weeks of gradual retraining. We look for measurable functional progress rather than keeping you in treatment indefinitely.

Will I have to come forever?

No. Dr. Adam's goal is empowerment, not dependency. We give you tools and active strategies to stay well independently. Some people stop care completely; others choose occasional check-ups because they enjoy how they move and feel.

What type of appointment should I book?

New patients begin with a New Patient Assessment. Follow-up visits are available in standard, 30-minute, or 60-minute sessions depending on whether you need hands-on care, pain education, or nervous-system coaching.

How do I know if Dr. Adam is the right chiropractor for me?

Dr. Adam may be a good fit if you want care that is hands-on, thoughtful, and collaborative. His approach works particularly well for people who want to understand what is contributing to their symptoms rather than simply being treated repeatedly without a clear explanation.
