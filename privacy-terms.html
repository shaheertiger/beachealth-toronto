Health Regulatory Standard

Privacy Policy

At Beach Integrated Health Clinic (Beachealth) and Beaches Foot Care Centre, safeguarding your Personal Health Information (PHI) is a foundational principle of our multidisciplinary healthcare practice. We strictly adhere to Ontario’s Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA), the Quality Care Information Protection Act, and Canadian federal PIPEDA privacy frameworks.

Collection of Personal Health Information

We collect personal health information directly from you or your authorized substitute decision-maker. This information includes your contact details, comprehensive medical history, symptoms, diagnostic imaging reports, physician referrals, and clinical progress notes recorded during treatments.

We collect this information solely to provide thorough clinical assessment, develop individualized rehabilitation programs, prevent contraindications, coordinate interprofessional care within our clinic, and submit direct billing claims to your extended healthcare insurance provider on your behalf.

Electronic Medical Records (EMR) & Cloud Security

All patient charts, intake questionnaires, clinical notes, and booking records are stored in encrypted form within SOC-2 Type II certified Canadian data centers provided by Jane Software Inc. Data transmissions use bank-level 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption. Only your designated treating clinicians and authorized clinic coordinators have access to your health records.

Circle of Care & Information Sharing

With your consent, your health information may be shared among the healthcare practitioners directly involved in your care at Beachealth (for example, collaboration between your physiotherapist, chiropractor, osteopath, and chiropodist). We never disclose, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties or marketing entities without your express written authorization, except where mandated by Ontario law or public health regulations.

Accessing and Correcting Your Health Records

Under PHIPA, you have the right to inspect, review, or receive a copy of your personal health records. If you believe your clinical record contains an error or omission, you may submit a written request for correction.

Designated Clinic Privacy Officer:
For any questions, records requests, or privacy inquiries, contact our Privacy Officer by email at admin@beachealth.com or telephone at (416) 546-4887.

Regulated Healthcare Professionals

All assessments and therapeutic treatments at Beachealth are delivered by autonomous, licensed clinicians regulated under the Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991 (RHPA) and their respective Ontario Colleges:

  • Chiropody: College of Chiropodists of Ontario (COCOO)
  • Chiropractic: College of Chiropractors of Ontario (CCO)
  • Physiotherapy: College of Physiotherapists of Ontario (CPO) via partnership with Javia
  • Massage Therapy: College of Massage Therapists of Ontario (CMTO)
  • Osteopathy: Ontario Association of Osteopathic Manual Practitioners (OAO)

Informed Consent

Prior to initiating any physical evaluation or treatment protocol, your clinician will explain the nature of the assessment, anticipated benefits, potential risks, and alternative options. You hold the right to ask questions, decline any part of an examination, or withdraw consent at any time.

Fees, Payments & Direct Insurance Billing

Payment for services is due at the conclusion of each appointment. As a convenience to patients, we provide direct billing for eligible plans through major Canadian extended health insurers (including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life, Green Shield, Desjardins, Blue Cross, and others). If an insurer denies coverage, requires a co-pay, or approves only a portion of the fee, the patient remains fully responsible for the outstanding balance.

Safe & Respectful Clinical Environment

Beachealth is committed to maintaining a safe, inclusive, and zero-tolerance harassment-free environment for all patients, clinicians, and administrative team members.

Clinic Scheduling Guideline

Cancellation & Missed Appointment Policy

Our practitioners dedicate reserved, one-on-one clinical time exclusively for your treatment and recovery. When an appointment is cancelled on short notice or missed entirely, another patient awaiting care cannot be accommodated.

24 Hours Advance Notice Required

We kindly ask that you provide at least 24 hours advance notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your visit. You can manage your appointments 24/7 through your online Jane App portal, or by calling our clinic front desk at (416) 546-4887 or emailing admin@beachealth.com.

Late Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice or missed without communication may be subject to a missed appointment fee up to the full value of the scheduled service. Please note that third-party extended health insurance providers do not reimburse missed appointment or late cancellation fees.

Unforeseen Illness & Emergencies

We understand that sudden illness, emergencies, and unexpected family circumstances occur. If you are experiencing an acute illness, fever, or infectious symptoms, please notify us as early as possible so we can accommodate your rescheduling with compassion and care.
