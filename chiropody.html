Dedicated Chiropody & Primary Foot Care

Chiropodists are primary healthcare foot specialists who assess, diagnose, and treat conditions of the feet and lower limbs. From painful ingrown nails and plantar warts to custom biomechanical orthotics and diabetic wound care, Beaches Foot Care Centre keeps you walking pain-free.

How Chiropody Care Works

Your chiropodist begins with a thorough assessment of your feet, footwear and walking, then recommends treatment based on what they find.

Treatment may include:

  • Biomechanical assessment & custom orthotics: Assessing how your feet and legs move, and prescribing orthotics made for your feet when they will help.
  • Nail care & ingrown toenails: Conservative treatment and minor surgical nail procedures for painful or recurring ingrown nails.
  • Heel pain & plantar fasciitis: Treatment plans for heel and arch pain, including footwear advice, stretching and orthotics.
  • Corns, calluses & skin conditions: Safe reduction of painful corns and calluses, plus care for warts and other skin concerns.
  • Diabetic foot care: Screening and ongoing care to help keep feet healthy and prevent complications.

Conditions We Commonly Help With

  • Heel pain and plantar fasciitis
  • Ingrown toenails
  • Thickened or fungal nails
  • Corns and calluses
  • Plantar warts
  • Bunions and toe deformities
  • Flat feet and high arches
  • Diabetic foot concerns

Foot pain is not something you just have to live with. If walking, standing or exercise hurts, an assessment is a good place to start.

Custom Orthotics, Made for You

Not everyone needs orthotics. When they are a good fit, your chiropodist uses a biomechanical assessment and gait evaluation to design orthotics around your feet, footwear and activities — not an off-the-shelf insert.

If you plan to claim orthotics through insurance, check your plan first: many plans have specific requirements, such as a prescription or assessment by a regulated foot specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a chiropodist?

A chiropodist is a regulated primary healthcare professional who specializes in assessing, diagnosing and treating foot and lower-limb conditions.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with Beaches Foot Care Centre. Some insurance plans ask for a referral for certain services, so check your plan if you are claiming.

Is chiropody covered?

Chiropody is commonly covered by extended health benefit plans. Coverage for visits and orthotics varies, so check with your provider.

What should I bring?

Bring the shoes you wear most often, any current orthotics or inserts, and a list of your medications.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our chiropodists care for patients of all ages.
