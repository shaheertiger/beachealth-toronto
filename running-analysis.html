Running Analysis at Beachealth: Run Stronger, Faster & Injury-Free

Many running injuries keep coming back because the underlying biomechanical loading patterns remain unchanged. Our running gait analysis uses multi-angle video assessment to evaluate stride efficiency, cadence, foot strike dynamics, and hip and pelvis stabilization — giving you clear, actionable strategies to reduce pain and enhance performance.

How Running Analysis Works

We combine a functional movement assessment with slow-motion video of you running, transforming biomechanical findings into focused cues and personalized drills.

Comprehensive assessment includes:

  • Multi-angle slow-motion video: High-speed recording of your stride from posterior, lateral, and anterior perspectives.
  • Cadence, stride & foot strike: Analyzing step rate, overstriding risks, and initial ground contact mechanics.
  • Hip, pelvis & knee control: Detecting Trendelenburg hip drop, dynamic knee valgus, and rotational compensations.
  • Footwear & orthotic evaluation: Impartial recommendations on shoe drop, cushioning categories, and custom orthotic suitability.
  • Strength & mobility drill plan: Targeted active rehab exercises to address weak links and protect joints.

Conditions We Commonly Help With

  • Recurring running injuries & overuse strains
  • Patellofemoral knee pain & IT band friction
  • Medial tibial stress syndrome (shin splints)
  • Achilles tendinopathy & persistent calf tightness
  • Plantar fasciitis & heel strike pain
  • Hip impingement & lower back stiffness
  • Safe return to running post-injury or surgery
  • Marathon and half-marathon race preparation

You do not need to be currently injured to benefit — proactive gait analysis helps fine-tune running economy and safeguard your body before race training blocks.

Who It’s For

Running analysis is invaluable for new runners establishing healthy mechanics, experienced runners aiming for personal records, and anyone whose training is consistently derailed by familiar aches.

Because Beachealth is an integrated multidisciplinary clinic, any biomechanical findings can directly connect with our osteopaths, chiropractors, physiotherapists, massage therapists, or chiropodists under one cohesive roof.

What to Expect at Your Visit

1 Clinical History & Training Goals

We review your current mileage, past injury history, training schedule, and footwear wear patterns.

2 Movement Screen & Video Capture

Joint range of motion and functional single-leg control tests, followed by slow-motion treadmill video analysis.

3 Video Breakdown & Action Plan

Frame-by-frame review with immediate real-time gait cues, footwear guidance, and home corrective drills.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my assessment?

Bring your primary running shoes and wear comfortable athletic running apparel. Shorts are recommended so knee tracking and tibial alignment are clearly visible on video.

Do I need to be in active pain to benefit?

Not at all. Biomechanical gait analysis is just as valuable for injury prevention, running economy, and transitioning to new mileage goals as it is for rehab.

Will I have to completely rebuild my running style?

No. We pinpoint the one or two high-yield mechanical adjustments that reduce tissue stress, gradually integrating them without disrupting your natural cadence.

Can I get orthotic recommendations from this?

Yes. If foot pronation or structural alignment indicates custom orthotic support, we can directly coordinate a full assessment with our onsite chiropodists.
