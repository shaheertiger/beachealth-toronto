Clinical Pilates

Beachealth Services > Clinical Pilates

Individualized Movement Rehabilitation & Clinical Pilates

Clinical Pilates combines Pilates-based movements with physiotherapy principles to improve strength, mobility, posture, balance, and movement control. Our focus is on individualized, goal-oriented exercise programs that support rehabilitation, injury prevention, and long-term physical well-being.

Meet Our Clinical Pilates Instructor

Expert guidance in mindful movement, postural rehabilitation, and core stability:

Adriana Lis Pereira

Clinical Pilates Instructor & Physiotherapy Assistant

Adriana brings over two decades of physical rehabilitation and movement education experience. She specializes in customized, reformer-based clinical Pilates tailored to recovery, spinal stabilization, posture, and core strength.
