Clinical Pilates

Beachealth Services > Clinical Pilates

Individualized Movement Rehabilitation & Clinical Pilates

Clinical Pilates combines Pilates-based movements with physiotherapy principles to improve strength, mobility, posture, balance, and movement control.

Our focus is on individualized, goal-oriented exercise programs that support rehabilitation, injury prevention, and long-term physical well-being.

Services Can Include:

Clinical & Therapeutic Pilates
Pilates for Strength & Mobility
Postural Re-education
Core Stability & Functional Strengthening
Breathing Techniques & Breath Control
Injury Recovery & Prevention
Supervision & Booking Options

Clinical Pilates delivered as physiotherapy treatment requires an initial physiotherapy assessment and an individualized treatment plan. Our physiotherapy assistants can then guide clients through their prescribed exercises under physiotherapist supervision.

Clients can also choose to book private sessions with Lis
