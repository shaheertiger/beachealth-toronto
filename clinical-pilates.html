Who Is Clinical Pilates For?

Clinical Pilates is suitable for individuals recovering from injury, managing pain, improving physical function, or looking to build strength and confidence in their movements.

It can help with:

  • Back and neck pain: Improving spinal mobility, postural awareness, and muscular endurance.
  • Sports and repetitive strain injuries: Supporting rehabilitation and a gradual return to physical activity.
  • Hip, knee, and ankle concerns: Improving joint stability, strength, and movement control.
  • Pregnancy and postpartum recovery: Supporting physical changes during pregnancy and rebuilding strength following childbirth.
  • Pelvic floor concerns: Specialized pelvic health physiotherapy consultations are available for individuals experiencing pelvic floor weakness, urinary leakage, or coordination difficulties.
  • Diastasis recti: Supporting abdominal wall rehabilitation and functional core strength.
  • Arthritis, stiffness, and hypermobility: Improving muscular support, mobility, and joint control.
  • Running and fitness-related injuries: Supporting rehabilitation and a safe return to sport.
  • Healthy aging: Maintaining balance, strength, mobility, and independence.

What Is the Difference Between Regular Pilates and Clinical Pilates?

Regular Pilates focuses on general fitness, flexibility, strength, and body awareness.

Clinical Pilates takes a more individualized, therapeutic approach.

At Beach Health, our physiotherapists assess your movement, strength, mobility, and functional limitations before determining whether Pilates-based exercises would benefit your rehabilitation.

Exercises are selected and modified according to your individual condition, physical abilities, and treatment goals. For clients with pelvic health concerns, our team can also arrange a consultation with pelvic health physiotherapist to explore appropriate treatment options.

Why Choose Clinical Pilates at Beach Health?

Clients benefit from individualized physiotherapy care with access to additional pelvic health expertise when needed.

Your physiotherapy treatment may incorporate exercises focusing on:

  • Core strength and spinal stability
  • Posture and body alignment
  • Hip and pelvic stability
  • Balance and coordination
  • Flexibility and controlled mobility
  • Functional movement & injury rehab
  • Return to running, fitness & daily activities

Our physiotherapists prescribe and progress Pilates-based exercises based on your assessment findings, ensuring that each movement serves a therapeutic purpose.

Specialized Pelvic Health Physiotherapy Consultations

Interested in learning more about your pelvic health?

Through our partnership with Javia, Beach Health clients can also access consultations with a Registered Physiotherapist specializing in pelvic health. These consultations may be beneficial for individuals experiencing:

  • Pregnancy-related pelvic or lower back discomfort
  • Postpartum recovery concerns
  • Urinary incontinence or pelvic floor dysfunction
  • Pelvic pain or discomfort
  • Diastasis recti
  • Difficulties returning to exercise after childbirth
  • Pelvic health changes associated with menopause

Our pelvic health physiotherapist can provide individualized assessments, education, and treatment recommendations based on your needs. Ask our team about arranging a pelvic health physiotherapy consultation.

The Benefits of Clinical Pilates

  • Improved core strength and muscular endurance
  • Greater mobility and flexibility
  • Improved balance and coordination
  • Better posture and movement awareness
  • Reduced movement-related pain and discomfort
  • Improved joint stability and movement control
  • Increased confidence returning to exercise
  • Support for long-term physical health and injury prevention

Individual outcomes vary depending on the condition and treatment plan.
