Beachealth Services > Physiotherapy

Dedicated Physiotherapy & Movement-Focused Care

Beachealth has partnered with Javia, a physiotherapy and movement-focused healthcare company dedicated to helping people move better, recover from injuries, build strength, and maintain an active, healthy lifestyle.

Meet Our Physiotherapists

Our registered clinicians from Javia provide thorough clinical evaluations and individualized treatment plans:

Shakiba Banifatemi

Pelvic Physiotherapist

Shakiba specializes in pelvic health, running-related injuries, and movement rehabilitation. As a physiotherapist and passionate runner, she combines clinical knowledge with her personal experience in running, strength training, and Pilates to help clients recover, prevent injuries, and remain active throughout life.

Amin Montazeri

Registered Physiotherapist

Amin focuses on musculoskeletal and orthopaedic physiotherapy, helping clients manage pain, recover from injuries, and restore movement and function. His treatment approach incorporates therapeutic exercise, manual therapy, dry needling, and electro-acupuncture when appropriate.

Our Physiotherapy Services at Beach Health Include:

1 Musculoskeletal & Orthopaedic Physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment of muscle, joint, and sports-related injuries, chronic pain, and movement limitations.

2 Pre-Operative & Post-Operative Rehabilitation

Individualized rehabilitation programs to improve strength, mobility, and physical function before surgery and support safe recovery and return to daily activities following surgery.

3 Pelvic Health Physiotherapy

Specialized care for pelvic floor dysfunction, prenatal and postnatal recovery, and women's pelvic health.

4 Running Assessment & Rehabilitation

Running gait assessment, injury prevention, rehabilitation, and return-to-running programs.

5 Dry Needling & Electro-Acupuncture

Treatment approaches offered when clinically appropriate to address deep trigger points and restore muscle function.

6 Strength, Mobility & Injury Prevention

Individualized exercise programs to improve movement, function, and long-term physical health.
