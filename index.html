About Our Clinic

Welcome to Beachealth!

A multidisciplinary rehabilitation clinic located in the heart of The Beaches, East Toronto.

We provide integrated healthcare through Osteopathy, Chiropractic, Physiotherapy, Massage Therapy, Chiropody, and specialized treatments including 3D Running Analysis, Radial Shockwave Therapy, and Physio Pilates.

Our practitioners work as a coordinated team. Your Osteopathic Manual Practitioner, Chiropodist, Physiotherapist, Chiropractor, and Massage Therapist communicate about your care, share diagnostic findings, and adapt treatments based on what is genuinely working for your body.

Experience and Integrated Team

Healthcare works best when practitioners talk to each other. We dismantle medical silos by co-managing your condition across disciplines under one roof.

Trained and Registered Practitioners

Every therapist is fully registered with their respective Ontario Colleges (CMTO, CCO, College of Chiropodists) and brings diverse international credentials.

Root-Cause Personalized Treatment

We do not offer generic 10-minute symptom relief. We identify why your lower back, neck, or heel keeps breaking down and fix the biomechanical origin.
