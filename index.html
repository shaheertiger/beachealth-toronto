Welcome to Beachealth!

A multidisciplinary rehabilitation clinic located in the heart of The Beaches, East Toronto.

We deliver comprehensive, patient-focused rehabilitation combining trusted clinical therapies with specialized advanced care—including Physiotherapy & Clinical Pilates and Radial Shockwave Therapy.

Our practitioners collaborate closely as a unified healthcare team. Instead of fragmented appointments, your clinicians share diagnostic insights, communicate directly under one roof, and continuously tailor your treatment plan to how your body is responding and healing.

Why Choose Us?

Experience and Integrated Team

Our practitioners communicate directly across disciplines under one roof—ensuring your Osteopath, Chiropodist, Chiropractor, and Massage Therapist align on your recovery.

Trained and Registered Practitioners

Every clinician is registered with their respective Ontario professional Colleges (CCO, CMTO, COCOO, OAOO), guaranteeing medical standards and insurance coverage.
